--- title: Research – Yuval Amit heading: Research description: Research and papers of Yuval Amit, undergraduate mathematician at the University of Maryland ---

I'm interested in algebraic number theory and arithmetic geometry, but as a young mathematician that's always subject to change :)

Papers

  • Product and Quotient Set Dominance in Finite Groups – Preprint With Lucas Chen, Christopher Housholder, Joshua Im, Joshua Khan, Steven J. Miller, Mateo Palomares, and Devayani Pradhan We proved a criterion for when a random subset of a group is more likely to have a larger product-set or quotient-set. This resolved a conjecture by Miller and Vissuet.
  • Breaking Universality in the Lower Order Terms in the 1-level and 2-level Density of Holomorphic Cusp Newforms – Preprint With Lucas Chen, Lawrence Dillon, Christopher Housholder, Xiaoyao Huang, Joshua Khan, Say-Yeon Kwon, Meiling Laurence, Steven J. Miller, Vishal Muthuvel, Devayani Pradhan, Luke Rowen, Pramana Saldin, Connor Yau, and Steven Zanetti We proved that the lower-order terms of a density heuristic for the low-lying zeros of families of cuspidal newforms have differing behavior depending on the arithmetic of the family, unlike the higher order terms which follow the "universal" Katz-Sarnak behavior.
