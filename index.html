Papers

  • Product and Quotient Set Dominance in Finite Groups – Preprint With Lucas Chen, Christopher Housholder, Joshua Im, Joshua Khan, Steven J. Miller, Mateo Palomares, and Devayani Pradhan. We proved a criterion for when a random subset of a group is more likely to have a larger product-set or quotient-set. This resolved a conjecture by Miller and Vissuet.
